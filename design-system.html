The Goal & Mission

The Challenge

As teams grow and products evolve, inconsistencies start creeping in, colors don’t match, buttons behave differently, and accessibility often feels like an afterthought. It becomes harder to keep everyone on the same page, leading to a fragmented user experience that doesn’t reflect the brand or meet user expectations.

I spend too much time reinventing the wheel, developers face duplicate effort building the same components, and the whole process feels slower and less efficient. On top of that, scaling without a clear structure makes it nearly impossible to innovate quickly or maintain quality across the board.

The Approach

To solve this, we set out to create a design system that acts as a single source of truth for everyone - designers, developers, and stakeholders. It’s more than just a library of components; it’s a shared language that brings clarity and consistency to everything we build.

Drawing inspiration from the Fibonacci sequence, we’ve applied its natural proportions to layouts, spacing, and visual hierarchy, giving the design a sense of balance and flow that feels intuitive to users. We’ve baked accessibility into every part of the system, so inclusivity isn’t an afterthought.

By collaborating across teams and building with scalability in mind, the system not only helps us move faster but also ensures every product feels connected, polished, and ready to meet users’ needs.

Spacing and Sizing

Introducing Fibonacci numbers into the Design System was a great experiment, and it worked particularly well for spacing and sizing. The natural proportions brought balance and harmony to layouts, creating a consistent yet flexible structure. It added an extra layer of thoughtfulness to the design while maintaining scalability.

Icons

The icons in the Design System are crafted to be simple, intuitive, and universally recognizable. Designed with a consistent stroke weight and geometric precision, they align seamlessly with the overall visual style. Each icon is optimized for clarity at various sizes, ensuring accessibility and effective communication across all touchpoints.

Navigation

Primary and secondary headers, tabs, links, the navigation panel, and other elements that guide users through the system have been consolidated into the navigation component group. This grouping ensures a consistent and structured approach to navigation, improving usability and enhancing the overall user experience.

Data Vizualization

The design system also includes a data visualization components, providing key building blocks for creating clear, user-friendly dashboards. Support for both light and dark modes, along with a curated warm and cool color palette, allows visualizations to remain accessible and visually balanced across different themes.

Design System in Action: Featured Projects


Stock Exchange App

This project redesigned the DLR Group Stock App into a real-time, user-friendly platform that supports employee-owners with clear investment tracking, Schwab integration, and admin tools to manage stock exchange operations.









INDEPRO App

INDEPRO is DLR Group’s internal project management portal designed to support integrated design workflows and project execution. It facilitates task initiation, resource planning, and collaboration across teams, serving as a centralized hub for managing project activities and aligning with the firm’s commitment to elevating the human experience through design.











Appointments App

At DLR Group, the Appointments app supports our annual employee-owner-led nominations process. Used by Associates, Senior Associates, Principals, and Senior Principals, the tool allows leaders to review colleagues for advancement by viewing profiles, past achievements, core values alignment, and peer feedback.

The app streamlines the nomination and evaluation process, helping ensure that promotions reflect our shared values and the contributions of our people.









Contracts App

DLR Group’s Contract Management System (CMS) is an internal tool designed to streamline how contracts are managed across architecture and design projects. It serves as a centralized hub for storing, tracking, and reviewing all contract documents—organized by project prime number. The CMS enhances coordination between project administrators, legal, and accounting teams, ensuring a structured approval process and improved visibility into contract status and details.
